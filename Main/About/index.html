About LibPhyMT

LibPhyMT — Library of Physics and Mathematics (it is a misnomer; I should have called it a Book) — is currently a personal educational site, though perhaps in the future it will become more than just a personal project. The goal of this site is to help its readers in their learning process, particularly in advanced mathematics and physics, while also allowing them to simply enjoy it as entertainment.

The site is a collection of lessons and simulations on various topics in physics and mathematics. The lessons are mostly focused on advanced mathematics and physics, while the modeling and simulations are intended not only to give the reader a better understanding of a particular physical system or problem, but also to provide the pleasure of building models and exploring them through simulation.

LibPhyMT is a work in progress, and I am constantly adding new content and improving what is already here. The site exists both as a part of my own learning and as a place where I can share what I learn with others. I welcome any feedback or suggestions, and I hope that LibPhyMT can gradually become a useful and enjoyable place for learning and exploring the world of physics and mathematics.

I am an independent student of theoretical physics and mathematics and the author of this site. Alongside building LibPhyMT, I compete (or at least used to compete) in physics Olympiads and participate in schools on physics and mathematics, such as ICTP PWF. I build physical and mathematical models and simulate them, and now I believe it would be nice to put some of them forward so that you may enjoy reading about the process and the simulations. On this site, I also provide lessons on things I have learned so far, with the hope that they will be beneficial to some of you.
