Support LibPhyMT

LibPhyMT is an educational website currently maintained solely by its author. The site is constantly under development, with new lessons, chapters, and simulations being added over time.

LibPhyMT was created to share mathematical and physical materials studied by its author, though only a small portion of these materials has been added to the website so far, along with simulations and models developed by him.

LibPhyMT deeply appreciates the support of its readers and visitors. If you are interested in collaborating with me, I encourage you to contact me.

Want to contribute?

If you have a good understanding of mathematics and physics, or experience using Manim, and would like to contribute content, suggest corrections, or collaborate in any form, please reach out through the Contact page. Whether it's correcting an error, improving a proof, writing an entire chapter, creating animations, or simply sharing feedback, every contribution is genuinely welcomed. I deeply appreciate your support and collaboration in making this project better.
